## Critical distinctions

- Aura is the displayed unit throughout; no token address, wallet signing, blockchain explorer, or crypto transfer is observed.
- Narrator calls the collectibles “NFTs,” but the UI alone does not establish on-chain ownership.
- Investment chart value moves additively with stake: A2,893.37 with own A7 → A2,948.37 with own A62 after A55 added → A2,886.37 after all A62 sold. This is evidence of an aggregate Aura content value, not proof of a bonding curve or token market price.
- P&L and investment badges exist, but the price/return computation is not revealed.
- Card opening appears a reward for claiming a daily quest/crate. No paid random crate purchase is demonstrated.
- Some blank videos, skeleton views, long loading, and a failed sale are visible. Robust caching, clear pending states, and predictable error recovery would make Clout materially smoother.
